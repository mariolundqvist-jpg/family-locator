A family locator that asks
before it shows.

Everyone in the group chooses whether to share, and can stop at any moment. An invitation lets someone ask to join. It never turns on their location.

  • Joining a family does not share your location
  • Only the last reported position is kept, and only for 15 minutes
  • No history, no hidden mode, and nobody can switch you on remotely

Testing is limited to consenting adults. Child accounts are a separate design with their own guardian controls and are not built here.

What is stored about you
  • One position snapshot per group — latitude, longitude, accuracy radius and the time your device captured it.
  • It is replaced on every update and hard-deleted after 15 minutes. There is no location history.
  • Pausing or stopping deletes the stored snapshot immediately.
  • Never collected: contacts, photo library, advertising ID, motion data, or a continuous track.

    Consent

    Turn on location sharing?

    This shares your position with the other people in this group. Nothing is shared until you press the button below.

    What will happen

    • Your device reports a position while sharing is on.
    • Everyone in the group sees your last reported position, its accuracy radius and the time it was captured.
    • It is replaced with each update and deleted after 15 minutes.

    What will not happen

    • Nobody else can turn this on for you, and no one can turn it back on after you stop.
    • No location history is built up. No advertising or third-party analytics.
    • You can pause, stop, leave the group or delete everything at any time.
